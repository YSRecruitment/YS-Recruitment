Who we are

A Yorkshire agency that knows three industries well.

We started YS Recruitment to do one thing properly: match good tradespeople, drivers and mechanics with employers who need them. We don't try to cover every sector. We work in construction, HGV driving and mechanics, so we understand the roles, the qualifications and the pressures on both sides.

That means we know the difference between a CPCS blue card and a red one, what a Class 1 tipper job really involves, and why a workshop needs a technician with their own tools on Monday morning. We check those details before anyone is put forward.

We're based in Leeds and recruit right across Yorkshire, from Bradford, Wakefield and Huddersfield to Sheffield, Hull, York and the towns in between.
